Academic Pathway

From Foundation to IGCSE

A structured progression designed to grow with your child

Foundation Stage
Years 1 – 4

Building literacy, numeracy, and a love of learning. English, Maths, and Science form the core — delivered at the child's own pace. At this stage, curiosity is the curriculum.

Development Stage
Years 5 – 8

Deepening academic knowledge across core and additional subjects. Independent thinking, structured study habits, and subject breadth — tailored around each child's strengths.

Advanced Stage
Years 9 – 11

Rigorous preparation for IGCSE examinations. Five subjects, critical analysis, and the academic foundation needed for sixth form, further education, and university.

Pathway to Qualification
IGCSE & Beyond

Structured preparation for internationally recognised qualifications — opening doors to universities across the UK, UAE, and beyond.
