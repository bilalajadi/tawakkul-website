A More Considered Approach

Your child deserves more than a seat in a classroom.

Traditional classrooms are designed for efficiency, not individuality: one teacher, one pace, one standard for many students.

Your child, with their own strengths, pace of learning, and character, is expected to fit within that model.

Many do not. Not because they lack ability, but because the system was never built around them. The Modern Governess Programme is.

Frequently Asked Questions
  • How is this different from tutoring?
    Tutoring improves individual subjects. The Modern Governess Programme replaces the school structure entirely, covering all core subjects as well as many other subjects, with a consistent teacher and a coherent academic plan built around your child.
  • Where is teaching delivered?
    Fully online, directly with your child. No classroom, no commute. Available to families in the GCC, North Africa, the UK, and worldwide.
  • How involved do I need to be as a parent?
    You receive regular progress reports and are always welcome to engage. The programme is designed to run without requiring your daily supervision.
  • How does my child's programme begin?
    A private 30-minute consultation, no commitment, no obligation, to understand your child's level and goals. A tailored plan is then developed specifically for them.
  • Is there a minimum age or year group?
    The programme is available for children in Years 1 through 11. We tailor the depth and pace entirely to the individual child.
