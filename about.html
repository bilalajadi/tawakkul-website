Founder & CEO

Mrs Muna Ajadi

Educator · Homeschool Coach · Visionary

Mrs Muna Ajadi is a British Muslim homeschooler, International Homeschool Coach, and the visionary behind Tawakkul Education. Together with her husband, she has successfully homeschooled six of her children — alhamdulillah — from birth, raising children who have memorised the Quran, are bilingual and trilingual, and have gone on to study at the University of London, Doctor Wait University, and City University.

One of her children became the youngest student ever accepted into HKBU University in Dubai — at just 16 years old — graduating with a First Class Honours Degree in Engineering in 2020.

Mrs Muna graduated from three UK universities whilst simultaneously homeschooling and classroom teaching. Her 20-year teaching career began in the UK, took her through 11 years in Dubai, then across the UAE — where she found her calling bridging Islamic values with structured, personalised home education for families across the world.

The Journey

From Classroom to
Every Home in the World

Tawakkul Education was not built in a boardroom. It grew from two decades of teaching in UK classrooms, Dubai schools, and private homes — and from raising six children entirely outside the system.

With a strong connection to both the UK and the UAE, Tawakkul is uniquely positioned to bridge British academic rigour with Islamic values — serving families wherever they are in the world. Whether you are in London, Dubai, or travelling between both, we meet you where you are.

This is not a course. It is not a platform. It is a relationship — built on trust, expertise, and a deep belief that every child deserves an education as individual as they are.
