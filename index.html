About Tawakkul Education

A New Standard
in Private &
Educational Placement

Tawakkul Education was founded on a single belief: that every child deserves to learn in an environment shaped around their nature — not moulded to fit a system built for everyone else.

We work with a small number of families at a time, pairing each child with an experienced teacher who understands values-based, personalised education. Our approach is calm, rigorous, and deeply intentional.

Our Guiding Principles

Built on Four Foundations

01
Personalised Learning

No two children learn the same way. Every programme is designed from scratch around your child's pace, strengths, and character.

02
Expert Teachers

We place only experienced, carefully selected teachers — matched to your child's specific needs and your family's values.

03
Islamic Grounding

Faith is not an add-on. It is woven into the environment, the rhythm of the day, and the relationships we build with each family.

04
British Curriculum

Academic rigour aligned to recognised frameworks, so your child's education is credible, structured, and purposeful.

The Governess Programme

Education That Fits
Your Family's Life

A fully personalised education system built around your child, your family, and your lifestyle.

  • One-to-one or small group lessons delivered by a qualified teacher in your home or online
  • Structured weekly curriculum aligned to UK educational standards
  • Regular assessments and progress reports shared directly with parents
  • Islamic studies and Arabic integrated naturally into the programme
  • Flexible scheduling designed around the family — not a rigid school timetable
