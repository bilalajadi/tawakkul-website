# Tawakkul Education — Brand & Business Direction

*Last updated: 16-06-2026 · Based on stakeholder sessions and website review*

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Summary

> **The Governess Programme is Tawakkul's complete home education service. It combines rigorous academic delivery with character development, critical thinking, and real-world skills — built entirely around your child and your family's life.**

This paragraph, confirmed by Muna as the essence of Tawakkul, should anchor all marketing, copy, and communications.

---

## 2. Who We Are

Tawakkul Education is a premium, personalised home education service for Muslim families in the UK. Founded by Mrs Muna Ajadi, a UK-qualified educator with **30 years of teaching experience** and **8 children homeschooled**, Tawakkul brings the standard of a private governess — individual attention, structured learning, character formation — to Muslim households.

**This is not a school. This is not school at home. It is something better.**

---

## 3. Positioning

| What we are | What we are not |
|---|---|
| A governess education service | A school or online school |
| Premium, exclusive, bespoke | Mass-market or generalised |
| Values-led and academically rigorous | Just Islamic studies or Quran tutoring |
| Aligned to the British curriculum | An alternative to all formal education |
| Built around the child and family | A one-size-fits-all programme |

**Reference point:** The standard of a British governess agency (e.g. Quintessential Governess, Randolphs Recruitment). Elite, discreet, deeply personalised.

### Positioning statement
*Elite governess education for Muslim teenagers — purposeful, complete, and built around your child.*

---

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary: Muslim families in the UK
- Parents who are dissatisfied with mainstream schooling (academically or values-wise)
- Families who want Islamic values integrated naturally — not as an add-on
- Parents seeking an alternative to overcrowded classrooms and one-size curricula
- Families with teenagers (Years 1–11) who need structured, credentialled education

### Secondary: Muslim diaspora internationally
- Families seeking UK-qualified education delivered remotely
- Parents preparing children for IGCSE and university entry

### Psychographic profile
- Educated, professional Muslim parents
- Value excellence AND values — they do not see a trade-off between the two
- Willing to invest in bespoke, high-quality education
- Looking for a trusted expert, not just a tutor

## 5. The Programme — Course Structure

### Year Groups & Tiers

| Tier | Year Group | Core Subjects | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Years 1–4 | English, Maths, Science | Core literacy and numeracy focus |
| Development | Years 5–8 | English, Maths, Science + additional subjects | Broadening academic base |
| Advanced | Years 9–11 | IGCSE preparation pathway | Internationally recognised qualifications |

**Note on additional subjects:** Exact subject selections are discussed on the discovery call — do not over-specify on the website. Show flexibility.

### IGCSE Pathway
Structured preparation for International General Certificate of Secondary Education qualifications. Opens doors to sixth form, further education, and university entry.

### What is included (across all tiers)
- Personalised lesson planning
- One UK-qualified lead teacher
- Character development woven into academic delivery
- Critical thinking and real-world skills
- Progress reporting to parents
- Islamic values integrated naturally (not as a separate subject)

## 6. The Name — "Programme" vs Other Words

**Decision (confirmed 16-06-2026):** Keep "Programme" as is. It is correct British English and appropriate for an education context.

**Words to avoid:** "program" (American spelling / connotations of programming minds), "placement" (implies placing a child externally), "online school" (too generic).

**Words that work:** Programme, pathway, journey, governess, bespoke, purposeful, complete.

---

## 7. Key Messages

1. **Personalisation** — Every programme is built around the child, not the other way around.
2. **Expertise** — UK-qualified teacher with 30 years experience, 8 children homeschooled.
3. **Values + Rigour** — Islamic identity and British curriculum are not in conflict here.
4. **Exclusivity** — We work exclusively with a small number of families at a time.
5. **Trust** — No commitment required. A 30-minute call is the first step.

---

## 8. Language & Tone

### Use
- "Governess education" / "the Governess Programme"
- "Home education" (preferred over "homeschooling" — less clichéd)
- "Exclusively" / "bespoke" / "purposeful"
- "Your child" / "your family" — personal, direct
- "Begin your journey"
- "No commitment required"
- Islamic greetings and references where appropriate (naturally, not performatively)

### Avoid
- "Homeschooling" (overused, everyone's jumped on the bandwagon — use sparingly or not at all)
- "Online school" (too generic)
- "Placement" (wrong connotation)
- "Programming" (wrong connotation)
- Corporate jargon
- Overclaiming ("best", "only", "revolutionary")

---

## 9. Narrative Arc (Customer Journey)

1. **Awareness** — Parent is frustrated with school; looking for an alternative.
2. **Discovery** — Finds Tawakkul via social media or referral. Website confirms premium positioning.
3. **Trust-building** — Reads about Muna's credentials, the governess model, the values alignment.
4. **Inquiry** — Books a free 30-minute call. No commitment required.
5. **Onboarding** — Discovery call → tailored programme → begin.

The website should serve steps 2 and 3. Everything else (social media, referrals, word of mouth) drives step 1.
